INDUSTRIAL INTERNSHIP • CONTROL SYSTEMS

RO Filter & Sparkling Water Machine

Designed the sensing and control side of a water treatment machine around a custom STM32H743IIT6 PLC. Interfaced an ADS1115 external ADC over I²C with pH and TDS sensors, and controlled 24 V solenoid valves through a MOSFET driver with flyback protection.

Prototyped the pH subsystem on an ESP32-S3 with a 20×4 I²C LCD, fixed ADC settings at 12-bit / 3.3 V, reduced noise using a median filter, and prepared P&ID-style block diagrams for the project report.

STM32ESP32-S3ADS1115I²CC/C++
